OpenMail

A free, open-source desktop mail client for Gmail

OpenMail is an Outlook-style desktop application for Windows. It downloads your Gmail into a standard Outlook PST file on your own computer, files messages into folders, and keeps your inbox tidy with rules you define.

  • Your mail stays on your computer. OpenMail has no servers. It talks only to Google's Gmail API, directly from your PC.
  • Standard PST files. OpenMail reads and writes PST files itself (Microsoft Outlook is not required), and the files open in Outlook too.
  • Rules and clean-up. Sort and group by sender, move or delete in bulk, and run Outlook-style rules against new and existing mail.
  • Open source. The complete source code is public under the MIT license.

How OpenMail uses Google data

OpenMail asks for permission to read, organise, send and move to Trash the messages in your Gmail account, so it can copy them into your local mailbox file, apply your rules, and send mail you write. It never permanently deletes Gmail messages, and it never sends your data anywhere except between your computer and Google. See the privacy policy for details.
